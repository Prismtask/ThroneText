# TerminalRPG — Ally Tier Lists (Updated)

> **Note:** All 46 monster girls now have innate skills and race passives. Tiers are ranked within the context of the current game systems (combat engine, skill scaling, and wedding special hooks). Lower-tier entries are still viable — these rankings reflect relative power and consistency.

---

## Tier List 1: Ally Rank by Skill Usefulness

Ranked by the **combat value of innate skills + race passive**. Every ally now has 2 innate skills.

| Tier | Allies | Why They Rank Here |

## Tier List 2: Ally Rank by Wedding Accessory

Ranked by the **combat power of the soulbound wedding accessory**, including the **Bonded** amplification (when the married girl is in the active party). The accessory is a Legendary item that the player equips — its effect scales with whether the source girl is in the party.

| Tier | Allies | Why They Rank Here |
|---|---|---|
| **S** | Cosmic Slime Empress, Dragon Goddess Avatar, Infernal Empress, Vampire Matriarch, Ninetales Fox, Lich Queen Avatar, Dryad Protector | Massive stats, game-wide damage, sustain, or enemy-crippling debuffs. These accessories define builds. |
| **A** | Scylla Wrecker, Gorgon Petrifier, Banshee Wailer, Crimson Countess, Siren Empress, Valkyrie Commander, Mermaid Siren Queen, Oni Bruiser, Draconic Valkyrie, Centaur Champion, Kitsune Miko, Neko Ninja, Arachne Brood Queen, Yuki-onna, Sphinx Riddler, Minotaur Gladiator, Demon Whip Master, Amazon Warrior, Mummy Princess, Dullahan Knight | Strong consistent effects: conditional damage spikes, healing, defensive procs, crowd control, or boss-killing power. |
| **B** | Ghost Maid, Vampire Seductress, Winter Fairy, Salamander Dancer, Slime Girl, Umbral Weaver, Lamia Constrictor, Arachne Weaver, Succubus Seductress, Kobold Tinkerer, Centaur Scout, Harpy Scout, Holstaur Brawler, Gargoyle Watcher, Lizard Queen, Mimic Girl | Good but situational or lower numbers. Useful niche effects (elemental absorb, blinds, slows, retribution). |
| **C** | Goblin Girl, Moth Girl Flutterer, Alraune Fledger | Weak or non-combat focused: gold bonuses, minor regen. Outclassed by combat accessories. |
